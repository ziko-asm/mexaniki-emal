Frequently asked questions

Questions and answers

What size parts can you machine?

Parts up to 1750 mm in diameter are machined on a vertical turning lathe — large parts that do not fit on a standard lathe.

What kinds of parts do you work on?

Flanges, rings, drums, gearbox and pump housings, wheel housings, plates and other large industrial parts.

I don't have a drawing. Can you still do the job?

Yes. The part can be made from an old part, a sample or from measurements.

How are price and lead time set?

Send a photo, dimensions or drawing of the part on WhatsApp. Master Telman Azizov assesses the job and agrees the price and lead time with you directly.

Where is the workshop?

Can I speak to the master himself?

Yes. Calls and WhatsApp messages are answered by master Telman Azizov himself, with no middlemen.
